  • Moved the dev environment from VMs to GKE Autopilot first, packaging all 10 backend services with one shared Helm chart.
  • Then moved the whole staging environment, backend and front ends, into the shared cluster, with namespace-scoped RBAC for the CI deployer and credentials in protected GitLab variables and Kubernetes Secrets.
  • Now moving production off VMs onto GKE with my lead.
  • Built GitLab CI/CD pipelines for dev and staging, with migration Jobs, immutable image tags and automatic rollback.
  • Managed GCP infrastructure with Terraform across dev, stage and prod: GKE Gateway, Cloud Armor, Workload Identity and IAM.
  • Cut non-production compute hours by about 64% by scaling workloads to zero outside working hours.
  • Tuned HPA autoscaling and health probes, and wrote k6 load tests for the platform and its Cloud Armor rules.
  • Self-hosted NVIDIA Nemotron on vLLM on a GKE GPU node, with a RAG proof of concept behind an internal chat UI.
